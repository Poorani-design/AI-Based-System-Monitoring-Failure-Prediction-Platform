What is .sort({ timestamp: 1 }) ?

👉 Sorts data based on time

Values:
Value	Meaning
1	Ascending (old → new)
-1	Descending (new → old)
👉 Oldest first → good for charts 📈
👉 Latest first → good for alerts 🚨
